Reference verifier

Check that every reference is real and correct. Finds references that can't be found, wrong details, and retracted papers.

Only the reference list is sent to Crossref and OpenAlex (free scholarly databases) to look each one up. Your paper's text is not sent.

How to read the results

Not found doesn't mean fake. Books, reports, theses, websites and some regional journals are often missing from these databases. It means: check this one yourself.

Details differ shows the record that was found, so you can compare the year, authors or journal. Matching is automatic and can occasionally pick the wrong paper, so always read the details.

Retraction data comes from the databases and may lag behind the latest notices. These are free public services, so very long lists may be slower.
