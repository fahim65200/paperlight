PDF tools

Make PDFs smaller, turn PDFs into Word files, and turn Word files into PDFs. Everything runs in your browser, so your files are never uploaded anywhere.

What to expect

Compress PDF. Light mode tidies the file's structure and keeps text selectable, but often saves little. Balanced and Smallest redraw each page as an image, which shrinks scanned and photo-heavy PDFs a lot, but the text can no longer be selected or searched.

PDF to Word. Editable mode keeps fonts, sizes, colours and alignment where it can. Pictures, shapes, table borders and coloured backgrounds are not carried over, and complex layouts like columns are simplified. Exact look keeps everything but as pictures. Scanned pages have no text to extract, so they are added as pictures. PDFs made with older Bangla fonts (such as SutonnyMJ) may come out as garbled characters.

Word to PDF. Works with .docx files (not the older .doc format). Headings, lists, tables and images are kept; custom fonts, headers, footers and page numbers are not. Pages are saved as images, so text in the PDF is not selectable.
