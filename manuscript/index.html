      What these tools can and can't do

      Word files are cleaned fully: the names you list are replaced everywhere in the text (including headers, footers, footnotes and comments), and hidden author details in file properties, tracked changes and comments are removed.

      PDFs get their hidden metadata and comment authors removed, but the visible text can't be safely edited here. The report shows which pages still contain your listed names, so you can fix them in the original Word or LaTeX file and export again.

      Always open the result and read it once before submitting. Self-citations such as "as we showed in Rahman et al." can reveal you even when your name is hidden, and those need your judgement.
