Good to know

Matching uses the first author's surname and the year, so "Rahman et al. (2020)" matches "Rahman, M., Khan, S., ... (2020)". Organisation names and their abbreviations, like "World Health Organization" and "WHO", are matched too.

Unusual formats can confuse it: names with particles, citations inside footnotes, and superscript number citations (which lose their formatting when read). Treat the results as a checklist to review, not a final verdict.
