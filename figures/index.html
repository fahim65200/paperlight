Good to know

Enlarging a small image sets the right dpi on paper, but it can't add detail that wasn't there. If a figure is flagged as enlarged, export it again from the original software (Excel, R, ArcGIS, QGIS) at a higher resolution if you can.

Figures are saved in RGB colour. Transparent areas become white in TIFF and JPEG. TIFF files are larger but lossless, which most journals prefer for final submission.
