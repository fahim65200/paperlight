How parts are detected

Sections are found from headings such as Abstract, References, Acknowledgements and Appendix. Captions are paragraphs starting with "Figure 1" or "Table 2". Using Word's heading styles, or putting headings in bold on their own line, gives the most accurate split.

Journals differ on what counts. Some include the abstract, headings or captions, and most exclude references. Check the author guidelines, then tick the parts that apply. Counts can differ slightly from Word's own count because of how hyphens and symbols are treated.
