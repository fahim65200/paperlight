About this index

Theses are collected automatically each week from the open repositories of Bangladeshi universities. Only titles, authors, years and links are stored; the theses stay on the universities' own websites.

For automatically collected theses, the field and topic tags are guessed from titles, so they can be wrong. "Hand-checked" theses were reviewed one by one.

Using a thesis well: study its structure, questions and methods, then write your own. Copying without citing is plagiarism.
